01 / Product Overview

Building Autonomous Safety Networks

The M50 is a cutting-edge, all-in-one GNSS monitoring station independently developed by Medo for critical monitoring in slopes, geological hazards, mines, and dams. Representing a new generation of structural health intelligence, the M50 features adaptive timeliness and precision, allowing it to dynamically adjust its operations based on environmental shifts.

Engineered for total autonomy, the unit integrates a high-capacity battery with built-in solar panels and utilizes advanced ultra-low power technology. By leveraging internal MEMS sensors and real-time displacement data, the M50 automatically switches between power modes, ensuring over 90 days of continuous operation under typical rainy or low-light conditions. With a 30-day proactive power alert system and on-device edge computation, the M50 guarantees data continuity and rapid response even in the most isolated and "zero-network" environments.

02 / Features

Key Functions

BeiDou Architecture

Powered by domestic BeiDou-3 chips for complete technical autonomy. Fully supports single-constellation positioning while maintaining compatibility with all major global satellite systems.

90-Day Adaptive Stamina

Features autonomous frequency-variable monitoring to achieve ultra-low power consumption. A single charge provides up to 90 days of continuous operation in typical field conditions.

Advanced Intelligent Analytics

Integrates multi-source data fusion algorithms for high-precision computation. Intelligent on-device analytics ensure that every data point is accurate, reliable, and actionable.

All-in-One Autonomous Station

Highly integrated design allows a single unit to function as a complete standalone monitoring station, significantly simplifying system architecture and reducing deployment footprints.

Rapid Field Deployment

The lightweight build and streamlined startup process enable efficient, single-person installation and maintenance, minimizing time and labor requirements at remote sites.

Industrial-Grade Resilience

Designed to withstand extreme environments with robust waterproof, dustproof, and anti-static protection. Operates reliably across wide temperature ranges with built-in surge defense.
