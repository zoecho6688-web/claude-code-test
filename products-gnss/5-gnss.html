A800 ｜ High-Precision 3D Choke Ring Antenna

The A800 is a geodetic-grade, four-system multi-frequency 3D choke ring antenna engineered for the most demanding high-precision monitoring environments. Designed to track BDS, GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo constellations simultaneously, it serves as a robust signal foundation for geodetic mapping, seismic research, and large-scale structural health projects. Its unique 3D architecture provides an unmatched barrier against multipath interference, ensuring that high-stakes monitoring in ports, dams, and landslide zones maintains sub-millimeter stability even in complex signal landscapes.

02 / Features

Key Functions

Sub-Millimeter Phase Center Precision

Achieves sub-millimeter phase center accuracy with exceptional stability and repeatability. This geodetic-grade precision ensures highly reliable data acquisition for bridge deformation, seismic monitoring, and landslide tracking.

Advanced 3D Multi-path Suppression

The unique 3D choke ring architecture is specifically designed to mitigate multi-path interference. By effectively filtering reflected signals, it maintains superior data fidelity in complex environments like ports and urban corridors.

Superior Low-Elevation Signal Gain

Engineered with high gain at low elevation angles to maximize satellite availability in obstructed terrains. Integrated high-gain LNAs support stable, low-noise signal transmission over cable distances exceeding 100 meters.
