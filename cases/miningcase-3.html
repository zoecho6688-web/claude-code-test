01 / Project Overview

Modernizing PG Monitoring for River Source Protection

Located at the critical source of a major river artery, this project serves as a vital cornerstone of the regional Headwater Protection Initiative, focused on the ecological modernization of a legacy phosphogypsum stack. The primary challenge lies in achieving a high-precision digital transformation within an extremely sensitive environment where there is zero tolerance for seepage, requiring the replacement of outdated analog sensors with a robust digital matrix capable of withstanding the site's remote conditions and the specialized stability demands of acidic phosphogypsum. By integrating subsurface deformation tracking and groundwater surveillance, the project establishes a "digital shield" that aligns legacy industrial infrastructure with stringent modern "Green Transformation" standards.

Medo’s solution facilitates this transition through high-precision, automated instrumentation that ensures long-term data integrity and rapid alert routing, providing a sustainable monitoring backbone that requires minimal technical maintenance while safeguarding the purity of the downstream ecosystem.
