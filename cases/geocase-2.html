01 / Project Overview

Orchestrating Regional Safety Across Three Provinces

Characterized by fragile karst topography, intricate fault zones, and fragmented limestone strata, these regions are inherently prone to sudden geological instability. The rugged terrain, coupled with extreme seasonal monsoon rainfall, creates a "perfect storm" for landslides and rockfalls, where traditional manual monitoring leaves dangerous blind spots in remote, inaccessible mountain ranges.

To address this existential threat, Medo launched a Provincial-scale Mega-project, deploying a massive digital monitoring matrix across the three provinces. This initiative encompasses over 580 critical-risk sites and more than 4,700 intelligent sensors, creating a unified "Digital Nervous System" for Southwest China. By orchestrating this high-density IoT network, Medo provides 24/7 synchronized surveillance, transforming the region’s reactive emergency response into a proactive, data-driven shield that protects millions of lives across thousands of square kilometers of volatile terrain.

03 / Outcomes

Outcomes & Impact

The project establishes a high-fidelity data pipeline that ensures the seamless, real-time transmission of geological diagnostics from over 5,000 sensors directly to provincial regulatory platforms. This massive digital architecture has fundamentally elevated the region’s early warning capabilities, transforming complex terrain data into actionable intelligence for authorities. By eliminating monitoring blind spots across the three provinces, the system empowers proactive risk prevention and rapid intervention, creating a resilient safety shield that effectively mitigates geological hazards in Southwest China’s most volatile corridors.
