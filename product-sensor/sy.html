01 / Product Overview

SY035 ｜ Smart Pore Pressure Sensor

Engineered for permanent embedment within hydraulic structures, concrete matrices, and deep soil foundations, this advanced piezometer delivers high-fidelity monitoring of internal pore water pressure alongside synchronous temperature telemetry. Forged with a rugged, full stainless steel architecture and an ultra-compact 24×125mm form factor, it effortlessly navigates extremely confined spaces. Supported by customized accessories, it adapts seamlessly to diverse geotechnical scenarios including standpipes and foundation boreholes. Driven by embedded smart identification capabilities, this sensor establishes a highly reliable foundation for structural health and automated engineering oversight.

02 / Features

Key Functions

High-Fidelity Vibrating Wire Engine

Leverages advanced vibrating wire technology to deliver an exceptional 0.1% F.S. measurement accuracy. The frequency-based digital signal output remains entirely unaffected by extended cable lengths, ensuring absolute data stability over vast engineering sites.

Synchronous Thermo-Pressure Sensing

Integrates a highly sensitive built-in temperature probe to enable continuous environmental monitoring. This multi-dimensional telemetry supports automated temperature compensation, drastically refining the accuracy of pore pressure analytics.

Hermetic IP68 Architecture

Constructed from premium corrosion-resistant stainless steel with strict IP68 waterproofing. It demonstrates formidable anti-interference capabilities, guaranteeing flawless long-term operation deep within high-pressure and heavily saturated subterranean environments.

Agile Multi-Scenario Deployment

Features a highly versatile structural design equipped with smart asset identification. The ultra-compact physical footprint accommodates effortless installation across complex terrains, from narrow foundation boreholes to rigid concrete structures.
