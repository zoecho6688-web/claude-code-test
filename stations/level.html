01 / Product Overview

MD-ML20 ｜ Smart Hydro-Geological Telemetry Hub

The MD-ML20 is a professional-grade, unattended sensing station engineered for high-precision mud and water level tracking in the most demanding field environments. This all-in-one terminal seamlessly integrates advanced ultrasonic or radar sensors with an intelligent data acquisition gateway and a robust solar power system. Equipped with a smart reporting mechanism and large-capacity internal storage, the ML20 ensures continuous data integrity for national geological hazard prevention, mining safety, and water conservancy projects. Its modular design and versatile interfaces allow for rapid deployment and seamless integration into large-scale, mission-critical monitoring networks.

02 / Features

Key Functions

Dual-Mode Communication

Combines Beidou satellite messaging with cellular backup (3G/GPRS/GSM) to ensure reliable data transmission even in remote areas with no conventional network coverage.

90-Day Battery Autonomy

Uses intelligent power scheduling and a high-capacity battery to sustain operation for over 90 days without solar input, making it well-suited for off-grid deployments.

Remote Monitoring & Configuration

Supports multi-center data push and real-time telemetry, allowing administrators to check sensor health and status remotely — reducing on-site maintenance visits.

Industrial-Grade Weather Protection

Built with integrated lightning protection and a ruggedized enclosure to withstand harsh outdoor conditions, ensuring stable long-term performance across demanding environments.
