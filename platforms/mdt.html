02 / Platform Functions

Six Core Modules

Explore the core capabilities driving our platform.

Real-time Data Acquisition

Real-time, multi-dimensional presentation of the overall operational status of platform devices, including device data reporting volume, device online status, device alarms, IoT card data usage, and more. Supports various display formats such as numerical values, line charts, bar charts, etc., and can be customized according to specific requirements.

Centralized Device Management

Monitor battery life, signal strength, and operational status for your entire hardware fleet from a single unified interface. Push OTA updates effortlessly.

Multi-tier Alert Workflow

Configure complex, multi-variable trigger conditions. Receive instant notifications via SMS, email, or webhook when thresholds are breached, ensuring rapid response.

Dynamic Visualization

Transform raw metrics into actionable insights using interactive charts, spatial heatmaps, and customizable dashboards tailored to different stakeholder needs.

Automated Reporting

Generate professional PDF or Excel reports automatically on daily, weekly, or monthly schedules. Streamline compliance and internal review processes.

API & Third-party Integration

Connect MDT to your existing enterprise systems. Our open RESTful APIs allow for bi-directional data flow with ERP, GIS, or custom digital twin platforms.
