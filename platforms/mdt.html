MDT

IoT Cloud Platform

More comprehensive management of the entire equipment lifecycle.

01 / Overview

Achieve comprehensive management through one single platform.

This intelligent IoT middleware platform integrates IoT, Big Data, and Artificial Intelligence technologies, comprising core modules such as device data access, message orchestration, stream computing, rule engines, business centers, and an open platform. Designed specifically for developers, O&M specialists, and project managers, it facilitates highly efficient device deployment and maintenance. Furthermore, the platform enables comprehensive monitoring of uploaded sensor data while providing a robust data backbone for diverse business applications.

02 / Platform Functions

Six Core Modules

Enable full-cycle management of equipment, data, and projects.

Command Center: Global Project Configuration and Visualization

Real-time, multi-dimensional presentation of the overall operational status of platform devices, including device data reporting volume, device online status, device alarms, IoT card data usage, and more. Supports various display formats such as numerical values, line charts, bar charts, etc., and can be customized according to specific requirements.

Product Management: Ecosystem Integration Administration

Engineered for extensive compatibility with both Medo and third-party hardware ecosystems. This module empowers product administrators to perform multi-dimensional queries and data parsing based on product categories, access protocols, and custom tags, ensuring highly efficient management of the platform's hardware templates.

Device Management: Unified O&M and Asset Administration

Delivers comprehensive, multi-dimensional control and operations for individual field units. It centralizes device querying and management, facilitating seamless asset transfers, command execution, and remote debugging. Users can monitor real-time device parameters and data streams, while maintaining full historical traceability for system logs and over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates.

Data Management: Intelligent Telemetry Analysis

Continuously analyzes device status metrics to instantly identify and trigger warnings for data anomalies. Critical alerts are immediately pushed to designated personnel. The platform empowers administrators with real-time flexibility to manage and adjust contact directories and notification endpoints, ensuring a highly responsive incident management workflow.

Early Warning Management: Real-Time Alert Routing

Generate professional PDF or Excel reports automatically on daily, weekly, or monthly schedules. Streamline compliance and internal review processes.

Rule Engine: Dynamic Automation Workflows

Empowers users with superior automated response configuration. By setting specific trigger parameters—such as data evaluation thresholds or specific device events—teams can automatically initiate corresponding actions to achieve customized automation. This robust architecture significantly enhances the cloud platform's scalability, enabling fluid, autonomous interactions across diverse business platforms.
