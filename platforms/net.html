MD-NET 3.0

Safety Monitoring and Early Warning Analytics Platform

Proactive Alerts. Assured Safety.

01 / Overview

Multi-Sector Predictive Analytics Platform

Independently developed on a robust IoT architecture, this enterprise-grade cloud platform is engineered to deliver comprehensive safety monitoring and predictive analytics across diverse industries. It features universal compatibility with over 60 types of monitoring sensors deployed in water conservancy, geological hazards, mine tailings, and digital infrastructure. Functioning as a unified digital hub, the platform seamlessly integrates holistic data management, intelligent telemetry routing, real-time early warnings, 3D visual analytics, professional outcome reporting, and rigorous access control to safeguard critical operations.

02 / Platform Functions

Five Core Functions

By integrating full-lifecycle monitoring management with instantaneous early warnings, the platform builds a highly reliable safety net to eliminate blind spots and avert costly damages.

Continuous Monitoring & Linked Alarming

Delivers 24/7 uninterrupted safety data services through a robust B/S cloud architecture and dedicated mobile applications. The system features a proactive defense mechanism driven by multi-tiered threshold evaluations and trigger-based linked alarms to ensure instant anomaly response.

Immersive Spatial Visualization

Transforms complex sensor data into intuitive visual insights. By fusing GIS mapping, real-scene 3D environments, BIM models, and panoramic imagery, the platform creates a unified digital twin that integrates multi-industry and cross-category elements into a single comprehensive display.

Deep Analytics & Professional Reporting

Empowers decision-makers with robust multivariate and multi-dimensional data processing engines. The platform automatically translates raw telemetry into actionable intelligence, generating highly detailed and professional data reports tailored for engineering compliance and safety audits.

Hierarchical Multi-Project Administration

Engineered for scale and enterprise security. The architecture supports the simultaneous centralized administration of extensive multi-project portfolios, reinforced by a strict, granular hierarchical permission framework designed for different enterprise roles and user levels.

Remote Diagnostics & Lifecycle O&M

Minimizes the need for on-site interventions by offering comprehensive remote device diagnostics and maintenance capabilities. Operators can monitor hardware health, execute troubleshooting commands, and manage the entire equipment lifecycle directly from the cloud hub.
