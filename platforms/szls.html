Digital Twin Watershed Platform

Bridging physical water infrastructure with dynamic spatial models.

On this page 01Overview 02Platform Functions 03Related Solutions
01 / Overview

Digitization of watershed physical information

Acting as a next-generation digital representation of physical river basins, this platform is built upon a robust spatio-temporal data foundation, powered by advanced mathematical models, and driven by domain-specific water conservancy expertise. By integrating cutting-edge technologies such as oblique photogrammetry, real-scene 3D modeling, digital simulation, BIM, and GIS, it establishes a highly scalable digital twin base tailored to multi-dimensional scenarios and diverse operational demands across the entire watershed ecosystem.

02 / Platform Functions

Four Core Functions

Whole-basin visualization display, equipment management, and early warning & prediction.

Watershed Digitalization and Dynamic Simulation

Establishes a high-fidelity digital mirror of the physical watershed environment. It enables dynamic predictions by simulating diverse "what-if" scenarios—such as adjusting precipitation levels or hydrological variables—to provide a rigorous scientific foundation for proactive decision-making and emergency drills.

Real-Time Telemetry and Disaster Management

Fuses continuous online monitoring with intelligent flood control and drought relief mechanisms. It provides real-time situational awareness and rapid response capabilities to effectively mitigate critical hydrological risks.

Intelligent Analytics and O&M Tracking

Delivers deep statistical insights into operational health by systematically aggregating data on system alarms, early warnings, field inspections, and maintenance work orders to drive proactive decision-making.

Lifecycle Engineering and Asset Administration

Centralizes the oversight of physical infrastructure and water conservancy assets. This module ensures complete visibility and optimal resource allocation throughout the entire engineering and asset lifecycle.
